08

Chinju no Mori

The village guardian forest
物語鎮守の森――集落の神社を包む、切られることのない古い森です。そびえ立つ杉は神の宿る場所とされ、人々は手を入れずに守ってきました。だからこそ、何百年も昔の姿のまま、いまも濃い緑が残っています。村を守る神と、その神を守る森。人と自然が交わした、静かな約束のかたちです。
The storyThe chinju no mori — the old, uncut forest that wraps the village shrine. The towering cedars were held to be where the deity dwells, and people guarded them by leaving them untouched. For that very reason, the deep green remains as it stood centuries ago. A deity that protects the village, and a forest that protects the deity. It is the shape of a quiet promise exchanged between people and nature.
