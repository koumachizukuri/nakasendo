Kitsune Kōyaku

The fox’s healing salve
物語落合に伝わる狐膏薬の話。傷ついた狐を助けた人に、狐が秘伝の膏薬の作り方を授けたといいます。その薬効は、医王寺に祀られる薬師如来の慈悲と結びつけて語られてきました。山里の暮らしの中で、信仰と医と、人と獣のあわいに生まれた一つの物語です。
The storyA legend handed down in Ochiai — the fox’s healing salve. A person who saved a wounded fox was given, in return, the secret recipe for a medicinal ointment. Its curative power has long been told alongside the compassion of the Medicine Buddha enshrined at Iōji. Born within mountain village life, it is a story living in the space between faith and medicine, between people and animals.
