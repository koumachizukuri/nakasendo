Masugata

The road that bends on purpose
物語宿場町の入口や出口で道がわざと直角に二度折れ曲がる――これが枡形です。攻め寄せる軍勢の勢いを削ぎ、見通しを断ち、町を守るための工夫でした。中山道を歩くと、今も石垣や曲がり角にその痕跡が残っています。平時には荷を運ぶ馬や旅人が行き交い、戦時には防御の要となった。一本の道に、暮らしと備えが折り重なっています。
The storyAt the entrance and exit of a post town, the road turns sharply twice on purpose — this is the masugata. It broke the momentum of any advancing force, cut off sightlines, and shielded the town. Walk the Nakasendo today and the stone embankments and bent corners still trace it. In peacetime, packhorses and travelers passed through; in war it became the key to the town’s defense. A single road holding both daily life and readiness.
