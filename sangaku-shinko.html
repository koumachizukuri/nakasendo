Sangaku Shinkō

Mountain worship
物語山そのものを聖なるものとして敬う――山岳信仰。険しい峰を登り、滝に打たれ、身を清めることで、悟りと再生を求めました。中山道の旅人もまた、行く手にそびえる山々に手を合わせたことでしょう。自然への畏れと憧れが、信仰の形をとった。山は、ただの障壁ではなく、祈りの対象でもあったのです。
The storyTo revere the mountain itself as something sacred — mountain worship. By climbing steep peaks, standing beneath waterfalls, and purifying the body, people sought enlightenment and renewal. Travelers on the Nakasendo, too, surely pressed their palms together before the mountains rising ahead. Awe of nature and longing for it took the shape of faith. The mountain was not merely a barrier, but an object of prayer.
