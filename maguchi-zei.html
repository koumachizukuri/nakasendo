Maguchi Zei

Why the houses are long and narrow
物語なぜ宿場の家は間口が狭く、奥に長いのか。それは間口の幅に応じて税が課されたからです。表通りに面する幅を狭くし、その分だけ奥へ奥へと住まいを伸ばした――いわゆる「うなぎの寝床」。税という制度が、町の形そのものを決めていったのです。今に残る町家の細長い姿に、当時の人々の知恵が刻まれています。
The storyWhy are the houses of a post town narrow at the front and long toward the back? Because tax was charged according to the width of the street frontage. People kept the face onto the main road slim and stretched the dwelling deep behind it — the so-called “eel’s bed.” A system of taxation shaped the very form of the town. In the long, slender machiya that remain, the ingenuity of those people is etched.
